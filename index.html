⚠ REPORTS ARE UNMODERATED AND PUBLIC

Anyone can post them to open Nostr relays. Eyemont only applies automatic filters (schema, personal-info filter, 24 h expiry, proof-of-work, community flags). Content may be false or offensive.

Turning this on connects to public relays to read reports. In this build, posting and flagging go to a local test relay only (nothing is published).

Source classes describe who owns or funds a source. They say nothing about whether it is true, accurate or biased.

OFFICIAL Government agencies, intergovernmental bodies (UN, WHO) and state-funded or public media (BBC, VOA, DW, France 24, Al Jazeera, CBC, ABC Australia…). Each outlet's funding is noted on its items.

MAJOR Large commercial outlets, networks and wires.

INDEPENDENT Independent, local and nonprofit outlets, OSINT groups, and citizen posts (Bluesky, Mastodon, Nostr reports).

The OFFICIAL / MAJOR / INDEPENDENT buttons stay at the top of every tab and filter the feed, NEAR and the map. GDELT items are classed by their linked domains. Unknown domains count as independent.

Linked incidents join items from all feeds that fall within about 25 km and 2 hours and share an event type (fire, shooting, crash, flood, storm…) or rare headline keywords. Sports and entertainment headlines are excluded. The sparkline shows items per 15 minutes over the last 2 hours. Confidence rises with the number of distinct sources, classes and layers. It is an automatic proximity match, not verification.

About. Eyemont is a free, non-commercial project: no ads, no accounts, no tracking. It shows events and places, never people. Headlines link to the publishers; BBC and Al Jazeera items are credited and unmodified, with the full story on their sites. Fire data: NASA FIRMS. Aircraft: adsb.lol re-api (ODbL 1.0). 911 data: city open-data portals under their licenses (e.g. Dallas ODC-By, Somerville ODbL). Code: github.com/SinDistrictMayor/eyemont.
